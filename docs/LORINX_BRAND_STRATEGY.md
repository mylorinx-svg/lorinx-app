# LORINX — Brand Strategy

## 1. Positioning

**For** people whose bodies work hard all day — at a desk, on their feet, in training — **LORINX is** the home-recovery brand **that** turns relief into a short, quiet routine at home, **because** every device is chosen one by one, explained honestly, and backed by a one-year warranty and Hebrew-speaking service.

- Category: home recovery and everyday wellness devices.
- Hero product: electric cupping device with heat and red light.
- Promise: *רבע שעה של שחרור, בבית, בזמן שלכם.*
- What LORINX is not: a clinic, a medical supplier, a discount marketplace.

## 2. Audience

| Segment | Situation | What they need to hear |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Desk workers | Stiff shoulders and neck after long sitting | A fifteen-minute habit in front of the TV |
| People who train | Loaded muscles between sessions | A tool that fits before and after training |
| People on their feet | Heavy legs and back after a shift | Relief without leaving the house |
| Busy parents | No time for appointments | No scheduling, no travel |
| Gift buyers / companies | Want a useful, considered gift | Warranty, service, bulk terms |

Traffic is mostly mobile and mostly from Meta ads, so the product page has to stand on its own.

## 3. Brand personality and voice

Calm, precise, warm, confident. Short sentences. Second person plural. No exclamation marks, no superlatives, no urgency, no medical promises. English is kept only for the brand name.

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| הזמינו עכשיו! | הוספה לסל |
| עד 30% הנחה במארזים | המחיר ליחידה יורד ככל שמוסיפים |
| מוצר מהפכני להפחתת כאבים | ואקום שמושך וחום שמרפה |
| לקוחות מרוצים ממליצים בחום | הודעות שקיבלנו |

## 4. Value propositions

1. **Primary:** a real recovery routine at home — fifteen minutes per area, in your own time.
2. **Control:** 12 intensity levels, controlled heat, automatic shut-off after 15 minutes.
3. **Simplicity:** cordless, rechargeable, three steps.
4. **Assurance:** free shipping, 14-day returns with no cancellation fee, one-year warranty, service in Hebrew.
5. **Curation:** a small catalogue, each product chosen deliberately.

## 5. Three creative concepts

### Concept A — Contemporary Wellness Editorial
- Personality: calm, cultured, unhurried. Colour: warm bone, ink, clay accent. Type: Hebrew serif display with a light sans.
- Photography: natural light, domestic settings, product on real surfaces.
- Homepage: full editorial hero, long-form story blocks. Product page: narrative first, purchase module second.
- Buttons: ink rectangles, underlined text links. Mobile: single column, large type.
- Marketing implication: raises perceived value; supports content and email.
- Pros: differentiates sharply from gadget stores; matches existing warm lifestyle photos.
- Cons: if taken too far, the purchase module gets buried and paid traffic bounces.

### Concept B — Modern Product Innovation
- Personality: technical, exact, engineered. Colour: cool white, graphite, signal red. Type: geometric sans, tabular numerals.
- Photography: product on seamless backgrounds, exploded views, spec callouts.
- Homepage: product hero with spec strip. Product page: gallery, specs, comparison table.
- Buttons: solid red, sharp corners. Mobile: dense, spec-led.
- Marketing implication: sells on features; invites price comparison.
- Pros: clear function, close to the device's own look.
- Cons: this is the visual language of every marketplace listing for the same device; LORINX has no proprietary specs to defend it; red CTAs repeat the current site.

### Concept C — Luxury Lifestyle Commerce
- Personality: aspirational, hushed. Colour: deep green or black with metallic accent. Type: high-contrast serif.
- Photography: art-directed sets, models, props.
- Homepage: full-bleed campaign imagery. Product page: sparse, image-led.
- Buttons: thin outlines. Mobile: image-first.
- Marketing implication: high perceived price point.
- Pros: memorable when the photography is there.
- Cons: LORINX has no campaign photography; generated imagery would misrepresent the product; a ₪190–₪800 catalogue with 7–14 day delivery cannot honour a luxury promise.

### Selected — Concept D, "Quiet Recovery" (A's atmosphere with B's clarity)

Editorial warmth for the brand layer, product precision for the purchase layer.

- Warm bone ground and serif headlines carry the brand; the buy box is strict, factual and fast.
- The red lives in the product, not in the interface. One oxide-red accent (`#9A3324`) for small details; the CTA is ink.
- Works with the photography that already exists, and gets better as real photography is added.
- Commercially safe: the purchase module is above the fold on mobile, the offer is easy to verify, and nothing depends on claims the brand cannot support.

Why not the most dramatic option: C would look best in a mock-up and worst in production, because the assets and the delivery promise are not there to back it.

## 6. Trust architecture

Trust is stated once per page, where the decision is made: three plain rows under the add-to-cart button (delivery, returns, warranty) with links to the actual policies. Supporting evidence: real customer messages, correct-use guidance, a clear "who should not use this" note, visible contact details and response hours.

## 7. Open brand decisions for the owner

1. **Reviews.** The live site shows "4.9 · 46+ ביקורות מאומתות" and named quotes that are hardcoded, with the same names carrying different quotes on different pages. They are not in the draft. If they are genuine, they can be re-entered in the theme editor (the fields exist). If not, they should stay out.
2. **Product title.** "להפחתת כאבים" is a pain-relief claim in the product title. Recommended: "כוס רוח חשמלית עם חימום ואור אדום". Changing it is a product edit and needs your approval.
3. **Price.** One unit is ₪237.33 in Shopify and ₪237.90 on the brand sheet. The draft shows whatever Shopify holds.
4. **Photography.** The packaging photo shows generic English branding ("Intelligent Breathing Cupping Massage Instrument"). See the asset brief in `LORINX_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.
